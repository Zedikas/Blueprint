# Privacy Policy for Blueprint

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Blueprint is developed by Samua Dezai ("I", "me"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

## Summary
Blueprint does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

## Data stored on your device
Your floor plans, including rooms, measurements, furniture, doors, windows, connections, names, labels and notes, are saved only in the app's storage on your device. The app also keeps a technical diagnostics log on your device to help find bugs. None of this is sent to me or to anyone else.

Your device may include app data in iCloud or computer backups, depending on your own iOS settings. These backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

## Camera
If you use camera measuring, the camera feed is processed on your device by Apple's ARKit to calculate distances. No photos or videos are recorded, stored or transmitted. The app only uses the camera after you start a measurement and after you grant permission. You can withdraw camera access at any time in the iOS Settings app.

## Exports and sharing
PDF exports, JSON backups and the diagnostics log only leave your device when you share them yourself using the iOS share sheet. Once shared, the privacy policy of the app or service you send them to applies.

## TestFlight and the App Store
If you test Blueprint through TestFlight, Apple may provide me with crash reports and the feedback you choose to send. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. I use this information only to fix problems and improve the app.

## Deleting your data
You can delete individual plans in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

## Children
Blueprint does not collect data from anyone, including children.
